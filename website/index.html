Questions students ask first

What happens after I finish a course?

You leave with projects, a clearer portfolio, and—if you take Freelancing, Remote Work or a platform course—practice with profiles, proposals or content. From there you apply or publish. We support the search; we do not guarantee clients, views or a job.

Do I need experience to start?

Several tracks start from zero: AI & Generative AI, Software Development, Web Development, UI/UX, AI Tools & Automation, Freelancing, and the content platform courses. Mobile, Cloud and Real-world Projects expect some programming first. Upwork, Fiverr and Freelancer.com work best with a skill you can already deliver.

Do I need a skill before the platform courses?

Upwork, Fiverr and Freelancer.com work best if you can already deliver a service (web, design, writing, automation). LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook can start with a clear offer or something you can teach. The ten skill tracks are still the foundation.

Will AI do the work for me?

No. AI drafts faster—profiles, proposals, captions, scripts, replies. You still edit, fact-check, film, talk to people and deliver the real work. Manually starting every task from a blank page is the slow way. Letting AI publish unchecked is the dishonest way.

Will HunarStack give me clients?

No. We teach you how freelancers find work, write proposals, price projects and deliver. That is different from placing you with clients. Platform courses do not guarantee hires, views or sales.

How is this different from a computer institute?

The goal is not a certificate on a wall. It is a skill you can practise, a project you can show, and a realistic path into freelance or remote work.

Why should I start now?

Your journey starts with one skill. Practised week by week, that is how people actually move. Learn today. Build tomorrow.

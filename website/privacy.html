How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: [date]

This policy explains how HunarStack ("we", "us"), operated by [legal business name], collects and uses your personal information when you use our website, apply for or attend our courses, or hire us for software services.

1. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, date of birth, city and country, education, occupation, course choices, emergency contact and messages you send.
  • Learning records: attendance, assignments, project work and feedback.
  • Payment references, such as the date and amount of a bank or wallet transfer. We do not store card numbers.
  • Basic technical data collected by our hosting provider, such as IP address and browser type.

2. How we use it

  • To process applications and registrations, and to run classes and issue certificates.
  • To contact you about your course, payments, schedules and support.
  • To respond to enquiries and deliver software projects.
  • To improve our courses and website, and to meet legal and accounting obligations.
  • To send news and offers, only if you agree. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate, such as website hosting and form handling, email, video-class and payment providers, and with instructors and mentors as needed to teach you. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

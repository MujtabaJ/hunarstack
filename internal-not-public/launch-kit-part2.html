Course syllabi

Each course ends with portfolio work students can show to clients. Adjust the tools to what you are comfortable teaching.

Python and AI Foundations

8 weeks, beginner, 2 classes a week
  • Week 1: Python basics, variables, input and output
  • Week 2: Conditions, loops, functions
  • Week 3: Lists, dictionaries, reading and writing files
  • Week 4: Libraries and calling web APIs
  • Week 5: Data analysis with pandas and simple charts
  • Week 6: Using an AI model API: prompts, responses, costs
  • Week 7: Build an AI tool, such as a document summariser
  • Week 8: Capstone: a small AI product for a real use case

Full-Stack Development

10 weeks, intermediate, needs Web Development or equal skill
  • Weeks 1 to 2: Node.js and Express, building REST APIs
  • Weeks 3 to 4: Databases: design, queries, connecting to your API
  • Week 5: Login, passwords, sessions and basic security
  • Week 6: Connecting a React front end to your API
  • Week 7: Testing and working in a team with Git
  • Week 8: Deployment, environment settings, monitoring
  • Weeks 9 to 10: Team capstone: a complete product with users and data

Freelancing Launchpad

4 weeks, all levels, 2 classes a week
  • Week 1: Pick a niche, define 2 services, set your pricing
  • Week 2: Build profiles on Upwork, Fiverr and LinkedIn, plus a portfolio
  • Week 3: Find jobs, write proposals, run client calls, agree scope in writing
  • Week 4: Deliver work, handle revisions, ask for reviews, get paid, win repeat clients
  • Bonus: Live proposal review, and a checklist for payment methods and taxes (check current local rules)

Client proposal template

Send this after the first call. Clear scope and payment terms prevent most disputes.

PROPOSAL: [Project name]
Prepared for: [Client name, company]   Date: [date]   Valid for: 14 days

1. Overview
[Two sentences on the client's goal and the problem we are solving.]

2. Scope of work
- [Feature or page 1]
- [Feature or page 2]
- [Feature or page 3]

3. Deliverables
- Source code and deployed website or app
- [Design files / documentation]
- 30 days of free bug fixes after launch

4. Timeline
Week 1: Design and planning
Weeks 2 to [X]: Development with a demo every 2 weeks
Week [X+1]: Testing, launch and handover

5. Price and payment
Total: [amount and currency]
[40%] to start, [30%] at the mid-point demo, [30%] at launch.

6. Not included
Anything not listed in section 2, including [hosting fees, paid third-party services, content writing]. Extra work is quoted separately.

7. What we need from you
Logo and brand files, written content, and feedback within [3] working days of each demo.

8. Next step
Reply "approved" to this email and we will send the invoice and start date.

[Your name], Hunarstack | [email] | [phone]

Video scripts

Each runs 30 to 45 seconds. Film on a phone, facing a window, with captions on.

BODY: Hunar means skill and craft. Anyone can copy code. Nobody can copy real skill. That is why we named our company Hunarstack: skill is the foundation, and the stack is what you build on it.

2. Three beginner mistakes

HOOK: Stop making these 3 mistakes when you learn to code.
BODY: One, watching tutorials without building. Build something after every lesson.
Two, jumping between languages. Pick one path for 8 weeks.
Three, waiting until you feel ready. Put your first project on GitHub this week, even if it is ugly.

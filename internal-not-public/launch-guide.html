Put your website online (Netlify)

Free to start, no coding, and about 20 minutes. Netlify also collects your enrolment form.

  1. Register your domainBuy hunarstack.com (and .ai or .dev if you want) from Namecheap or GoDaddy.
  2. Unzip your packageOpen hunarstack-brand-and-website.zip. The folder you upload is called website.
  3. Create a Netlify accountSign up at netlify.com, choose Add new site, then Deploy manually.
  4. Drag the website folder inYour site goes live on a temporary netlify.app address. Click through every page and the enrolment form.
  5. Connect your domainOpen Domain management, add hunarstack.com and follow the DNS instructions at your registrar. This can take from a few minutes to a day.
  6. Check the padlockNetlify adds HTTPS automatically once the domain works.
  7. Turn on form alertsIn Forms, open the enrol form and add an email notification so every application reaches you.
  8. Create business emailSet up hello@hunarstack.com with Zoho Mail or Google Workspace, then replace the placeholder email on your pages.

To update the site later, drag the changed folder onto Netlify again. Free-plan limits change, so check Netlify's current form and bandwidth limits. Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages also host static sites, but they do not collect form entries.

Set your course fees

Enter your own costs to find the lowest fee that covers them and a fee that gives you the margin you want. The starting numbers are examples only.

  • Look up three local academies and two international bootcamps that teach the same subject, and note their fees and what is included.
  • Offer three price points: early-bird for the first 10 seats, standard, and a payment plan in 2 or 3 instalments.
  • Give one scholarship seat per batch and a group discount for 3 or more friends or a company.
  • Price short courses (Freelancing, AI Tools) lower to create an easy first purchase, then invite students to the longer tracks.
  • State the refund rule in writing, for example a full refund before the first class and none after Week 2.

Enrolment form

Your package now includes enrol.html and thanks.html in the website folder. Once deployed on Netlify, applications are saved in the Forms tab and can be emailed to you.

  1. Applicant reviewsReply within one working day with the course details and a payment link or bank details.
  2. Confirm the seatMark the seat as paid in a simple sheet: name, course, batch, amount, date.
  3. Add to the groupInvite them to the batch WhatsApp or Discord group and send the welcome email below.
